What the card actually does — codified.

Every value below is lifted from the deployed business card. Where the card contradicted itself, the token carries a proposed tag and the conflict is listed at the bottom for you to rule on.

FeelSharp, dark, neon-lit. Casino signage, not SaaS. Color ruleText is never colored. Accents live in icons, edges, rules, the one gradient. DepthDeep tight shadows + 1px inset top highlight. Glows are rings + bloom, never blobs. MotionOne ambient cue per screen. Everything else is ≤.25s and quiet.

8 · Where the card contradicts itself — your call

  1. 01--gold is jade. The token named gold is #35e0a1. Kept as an alias so nothing breaks; new code uses --jade. Also the :root comment still says "NeonDragon Casino" — leftover name.
  2. 02Control border isn't a token. Every button hard-codes #4a4a54, except Get Directions which uses --line2 (#3d3d45). Proposed: --line3 = #4a4a54 on all controls.
  3. 03Hover lift varies. Buttons lift −2px; the review button lifts −1px; the flip control scales 1.09 instead. Proposed: −2px on all rectangular controls, scale reserved for the round control.
  4. 04Seven hand-written shadows. Card face, save, row, links, directions, review (in em units) and go each have their own recipe. Collapsed to four: card / control / control-hover / control-active, plus float.
  5. 0516 font sizes. 9, 9.5, 11, 11.5, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 28, 34, 44. Kept the 8 that carry a role (section 2). 12/12.5/13/14/16/17 fold into neighbours.
  6. 06Spacing is off-grid. Gaps of 9, 11, 14, 18, 22 appear alongside 8/12/16/20. Proposed 4px grid; 22 → 24, 18 → 16 or 20, 9/11 → 8/12.
  7. 07Five breakpoints. 520, 560, 720, 820, 900. 520 and 720 are one-off fixes. Proposed three: 560 / 820 / 900.
  8. 08Reduced motion is half-respected. The pulse and shimmer deliberately ignore prefers-reduced-motion. Defensible on a one-button card; on a public site I recommend honouring it everywhere.
  9. 09Logo SVG is empty. aurora_casino.svg wraps a raster <image> with no data — it renders nothing. Using the 1330px transparent PNGs (gradient + mono) and the 1024px badge instead.
