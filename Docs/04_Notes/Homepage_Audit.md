# Homepage audit — auroracasinomt.com (current Wix site)
Source: uploads/Casino _ Aurora Casino _ Billings.html (Wix Thunderbolt export, 531 KB). Audited 2026-09-04.

## Structure (top to bottom)
1. Header — Facebook + Instagram icons, nav: HOME · Players Club · Promotions & Perks · Gallery · CONTACT (CONTACT links back to "/") · "More" overflow.
2. Hero — full-bleed photo (bar, 1905×945 stretched from a 980×905 source), eyebrow "YOUR LOCAL CASINO", one paragraph of copy, a Facebook Page plugin iframe (370×622) beside it.
3. Contact strip — bar photo background, embedded Google Map, "CONTACT US", social icons, "(406) 601-1282 | contact us on facebook or instagram", "1918 Grand Avenue, Billings, MT".
4. Footer — empty container.

That is the entire page: one paragraph of copy, one phone number, one address, two embeds.

## Content inventory
- Copy: "The Aurora Casino offers casino thrills, beer & wine accommodations, and customer service that is nothing short of extraordinary. With a newly renovated, full-service casino we are committed to making sure your time here is comfortable, enjoyable and relaxing."
- Phone (406) 601-1282 (plain text, not a tel: link). Address 1918 Grand Avenue, Billings, MT (plain text).
- No hours. No age/ID notice. No mention of what machines, how many, what's on tap, food, events, players club value. Nothing time-sensitive.
- Photos used on home: 1 (bar). Gallery photos exist but live on a separate page.

## What a visitor is there to find
Are you open right now · where are you · what's going on tonight/this week · what do you have (machines, bar, taps) · is it worth the drive · players club — what do I get. The current page answers only "where are you".

## What's working
- The venue itself photographs extremely well — the bar photo carries the whole page.
- The logo and the neon exterior signage already establish a magenta/jade/violet identity; the site just doesn't use it.
- The nav labels are the right five destinations.

## What isn't
- Zero answers to the top visitor questions (hours, promos, what's inside). The promo content lives one click away on a page nobody is told about.
- Facebook plugin iframe as hero content — slow, often blocked, dead on mobile, and it hands the visitor to Facebook.
- Phone and address aren't tappable. On a phone that's the one thing people want.
- Typography is Wix defaults (Oswald / Raleway / Almoni / Museo all loaded, Arial fallbacks). No relation to the brand marks.
- Hero image is a 980px source stretched to 1905px — soft on any modern screen.
- CONTACT nav item links to the homepage. Footer is empty. Title tag is "Casino | Aurora Casino | Billings".
- 531 KB of HTML before a single image loads; ~15 external stylesheet/script origins.
- Copy is generic ("nothing short of extraordinary") and says nothing specific about this room.

## Implications for the new homepage
- Home must answer hours / where / what's on this week / what's inside above the fold on a phone.
- Tap-to-call, tap-for-directions as first-class controls (card pattern).
- Real photos, big, under the scrim. The gallery is the pitch.
- Promos surfaced on home as a weekly strip (card already has this pattern), not buried.
- Players club as a value proposition, not a nav label.
- One page, static, fast. No third-party embeds except an inverted map (card pattern).
