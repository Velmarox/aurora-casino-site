AURORACASINO SITE - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha → -beta at feature-complete → -rc1 when launch-ready → v1.0.0 gold
A bump renames the build root folder and adds an entry here; working files keep their names.
Archived (outgoing-version) copies live in _version_archive/, never in the build folder.

Folder organization (Firebase-native repo):
  public/            Hosting deploy root — index.html, Css/, Js/, Assets/  (lowercase "public" is Firebase's convention)
  Docs/01_Design_System/  tokens (Aurora_Tokens.css) + token reference doc
  Docs/02_Assets/         master brand marks + gallery photography (public/Assets holds deploy copies)
  Docs/02_Assets/Video/   hero video poster master (the .mp4 deploy copy lives in public/Assets/Video/)
  Docs/02_Assets/Promos/  promo graphics — not deployed until a promos page exists
  Docs/02_Assets/Team/    staff photography — not deployed until a team page exists
  Docs/04_Notes/          audits and decision records
  _version_archive/       frozen copies of outgoing versions — never edit
  firebase.json, .firebaserc, .gitignore, README.md, CHANGELOG.txt at the root

v0.0.1-alpha (2026-09-04)
- Weekday tag moved inside the copy column. It had been its own row above a vertically
  centred body, so a tall banner left a visible gap between the tag and the content.
  All five banners are now a uniform 196px instead of 275-372px.
- Tuesday: tier amounts moved into the description (Silver $10, Gold $20, Platinum $30,
  Platinum Plus $40); its chips are now 'Log in and get rewards' / 'All day long'.
- Weekly promos replaced with the current schedule from the Aurora Casino Facebook page
  (posts updated ~2026-09-01). Monday 3X Points; Tuesday Loyalty Rewards Day (Silver $10 /
  Gold $20 / Platinum $30 / Platinum Plus $40); Wednesday Win-It Wednesdays ($25 cash
  drawing, monthly $300, $1,000 year-end); Thursday VIP Rewards Day (Platinum tiers only);
  Friday Nifty-Fifty 7-9 PM, drawings every 30 min, prize by player rank.
  THERE ARE NO SATURDAY OR SUNDAY PROMOS — those two days render no banner at all.
  Wine Down Wednesday, Match Play Tuesday, Seahorse Races and the 2X point days are gone.
- Descriptions restored as a third banner column: copy | description | chips.
- draws[] now holds MINUTES past midnight, not hours, so Friday's half-hour drawings work.
  Added fmtTime(); fmtHour() still drives the open/closed sign.
- FIXED a live bug: the hero 'tonight' line read tp.t, a field removed in the previous
  commit, so it was printing 'undefined' on the deployed site. It now uses tp.when, and on
  weekends falls forward to the next weekday promo instead of breaking on a null day.
- Promo images renamed by CONTENT rather than day (Promo_Gaming_Floor, Promo_Cash_On_Bar,
  Promo_Drawing_Drum, Promo_Lounge_Wine, Promo_Slots_Night) so a promo change no longer
  leaves a filename lying. Draft_Taps and Neon_Lanes are unused and left out of public/.
- Homepage copy corrected where the old promos had made it false: meta description,
  the 'every single day' subhead, and three players-club lines citing dead promos.
- Promo banners: the standalone time stamp is gone; times now live inside the chips, so
  each chip is a complete statement rather than half a thought pointing at a number in
  the corner. Sunday reads '2X POINTS 8 AM - 12 PM', Tuesday '$5-$25 MATCH PLAY ALL DAY'.
  Days with a drawings row already carry their times there.
- Chips always stack vertically (no wrapping) and moved right into the freed space.
  Weekday tag enlarged to clamp(13px,1.15vw,16px), chips to clamp(12px,1.05vw,14.5px).

  max-width line in the mobile block.
- Promo chips moved out of the text column into the open middle of the banner (centred

  desktop, back to static and stacked under the copy below 700px.

  text colour with a shadow, since they sit over the brighter part of the photo.
