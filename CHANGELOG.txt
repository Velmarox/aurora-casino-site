AURORACASINO SITE - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha → -beta at feature-complete → -rc1 when launch-ready → v1.0.0 gold
A bump renames the build root folder and adds an entry here; working files keep their names.
Archived (outgoing-version) copies live in _version_archive/, never in the build folder.

Folder organization (Firebase-native repo):
  public/            Hosting deploy root — index.html, Css/, Js/, Assets/  (lowercase "public" is Firebase's convention)
  Docs/01_Design_System/  tokens (Aurora_Tokens.css) + token reference doc
  Docs/02_Assets/         master brand marks + gallery photography (public/Assets holds deploy copies)
  Docs/02_Assets/Video/   hero video poster master (the .mp4 deploy copy lives in public/Assets/Video/)
  Docs/02_Assets/Promos/  promo graphics — not deployed until a promos page exists
  Docs/02_Assets/Team/    staff photography — not deployed until a team page exists
  Docs/04_Notes/          audits and decision records
  _version_archive/       frozen copies of outgoing versions — never edit
  firebase.json, .firebaserc, .gitignore, README.md, CHANGELOG.txt at the root

v0.0.1-alpha (2026-09-04)
- Promo banners: the standalone time stamp is gone; times now live inside the chips, so
  each chip is a complete statement rather than half a thought pointing at a number in
  the corner. Sunday reads '2X POINTS 8 AM - 12 PM', Tuesday '$5-$25 MATCH PLAY ALL DAY'.
  Days with a drawings row already carry their times there.
- Chips always stack vertically (no wrapping) and moved right into the freed space.
  Weekday tag enlarged to clamp(13px,1.15vw,16px), chips to clamp(12px,1.05vw,14.5px).
- Removed the now-unused per-day 't' field and its .day .t rule.
- Promo banners: supporting sentence removed, title enlarged to clamp(24px,2.7vw,36px)
  to carry the banner on its own. Drawing-time row unchanged.
- The three free-play chips now read 'free machine play DRAWINGS' — they are drawings,
  not open free play. Thursday's 'Three drawings' and Friday's 'Nifty Fifty' chips became
  'Must be present to win' (from the original copy); both had become redundant.
- Removed dead .day .desc rules, a stale .day .body grid override and a duplicated
  max-width line in the mobile block.
- Promo chips moved out of the text column into the open middle of the banner (centred
  around 62% of the width, between the copy and the time). Absolutely positioned on
  desktop, back to static and stacked under the copy below 700px.
- Promo time stamps enlarged from 11.5px to clamp(15px,1.35vw,19px), bold, full-strength
  text colour with a shadow, since they sit over the brighter part of the photo.
- Promo banners restyled after the printed weekly-promos sheet: the day is a light box
  with black bold caps, and each promo's claims are now theme-coloured chips with black
  caps (jade for money/free play, magenta for point multipliers, outline for detail)
  instead of one sentence. A short supporting line sits beneath. Contrast measured:
  black on jade 11.0:1, on magenta 6.8:1, on the day tag 16.7:1 (AAA/AA/AAA).
  Chips have solid fills so they stay legible anywhere over the photo, which lets them
  run wider than the body copy and fill the banner.
- Weekly promos rebuilt as full-width banner rows: the day's promo photo sits behind the
  copy at 45% opacity (34% on mobile) under a horizontal scrim, so contrast never depends
  on which image is in play. Images lazy-load; 252 KB added to public/Assets/Promos/.
  Removed the 2-up/4-up card grid rules — banners are one per row at every width.
- Players Club content from the old site: Real Time Rewards and the no-earning-cap line
  added to the club pitch, plus a seven-item benefits chip list.
- Promo fine print now carries the actual house-rule wording: 18+ to wager, government
  issued photo ID may be required, promotions may be modified or removed at any time.
- NOT DONE: no game details added. All three saved Wix pages captured almost no content
  (Promotions & Perks is empty; the homepage yielded one paragraph), so there was no
  source for machine or game specifics and none were invented.
- Weekly promo banner set added (7 AI-generated backgrounds, one per day) under
  Docs/02_Assets/Promos/. Masters are full-resolution; Web/ holds 1800px AVIF deploy
  copies (237 KB for all seven). Not in public/ — there is no promos page yet.
  Standardized to 21:9 by trimming top and bottom; subjects sit right-of-centre so
  nothing was lost. Uncropped files were copied byte-for-byte to avoid re-encoding.
  Every image has a dark left third (luminance 11-31 vs 58-87 right) for text overlay.
  Promo_Fri_Nifty_Fifty is 1024x339 where the rest are 2752-3168px wide — it needs
  regenerating at full size, and was left uncropped rather than shrunk further.
