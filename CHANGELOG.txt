AURORACASINO SITE - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha → -beta at feature-complete → -rc1 when launch-ready → v1.0.0 gold
A bump renames the build root folder and adds an entry here; working files keep their names.
Archived (outgoing-version) copies live in _version_archive/, never in the build folder.

Folder organization (Firebase-native repo):
  public/            Hosting deploy root — index.html, Css/, Js/, Assets/  (lowercase "public" is Firebase's convention)
  Docs/01_Design_System/  tokens (Aurora_Tokens.css) + token reference doc
  Docs/02_Assets/         master brand marks + gallery photography (public/Assets holds deploy copies)
  Docs/02_Assets/Video/   hero video poster master (the .mp4 deploy copy lives in public/Assets/Video/)
  Docs/02_Assets/Promos/  promo graphics — not deployed until a promos page exists
  Docs/02_Assets/Team/    staff photography — not deployed until a team page exists
  Docs/04_Notes/          audits and decision records
  _version_archive/       frozen copies of outgoing versions — never edit
  firebase.json, .firebaserc, .gitignore, README.md, CHANGELOG.txt at the root

v0.0.3-alpha (2026-09-04)
- Removed 'On Grand Avenue with a free lot out front.' from the Visit heading and the
  'Come in and sign up' button from the players club, both at the owner's request. With
  nothing sitting beside the Visit headline the .sec-head .row wrapper had no job left,
  so that head is back to eyebrow + h2. Parking still appears in the hours table.
- .btn.positive removed with it. The club CTA was its only user, so the jade button
  variant was three dead rules. It is in git if a jade CTA comes back.
- FIXED: og:image was an AVIF, so every Facebook, iMessage, Slack and X share rendered
  with no image at all. None of those crawlers decode AVIF. Replaced with a 1200x630
  progressive JPEG (78 KB) at Assets/Brand/Aurora_Share_Card.jpg, cropped from the
  Hero_Poster.jpg master, plus og:image:type / width / height / alt so the crawlers do
  not have to fetch it to lay the card out. The JSON-LD image points at it too. This
  matters here: Facebook is where the promos actually get posted.
- FIXED: index.html was inheriting Firebase's one-hour default Cache-Control, so a promo
  or hours change could take an hour to reach a returning visitor. firebase.json now
  sends no-cache for html/css/js. Images and video keep the one-year max-age, which is
  safe only because promo art is renamed by content rather than overwritten in place.
- Added meta robots noindex, pre-launch only and commented as such in <head>.
  auroracasinomt.com still serves the old Wix site, so an indexed copy at the hosting URL
  would compete with the business's own listing. Delete that line at cutover.

v0.0.2-alpha (2026-09-04)
- Copy pass across the whole homepage to strip the tells that make writing read as
  machine-generated. Zero em dashes left in any visible string (they were in the title,
  the hero, the club pitch, both legal blocks and two promo descriptions); en dashes are
  kept where they belong, in time ranges. Also gone: the unearned superlatives ('the best
  snacks on Grand Avenue', 'the most underrated players club in town', 'a players club
  nobody talks about enough'), the contrast-fragment headline 'Newly renovated. Still your
  neighborhood spot.', the vague 'the good kind. Ask.' / 'the good stuff', and the closing
  flourish 'you're leaving money on the floor'. Every replacement states something checkable
  or says nothing.
- Removed 'Come see us - ask for anyone on the floor by name.' from the hero at the
  owner's request.
- 'player's club' corrected to 'players club', which is how the rest of the page spells it.
- FIXED: the drawing countdown ran on the wrong side of midnight. Between 12 AM and 2 AM
  the casino day is still yesterday's, but the clock had rolled over, so every drawing
  time still read as later today. At 1 AM Saturday the hero advertised 'Next drawing in
  18h' for drawings that had finished four hours earlier. Guarded on afterMidnight.
- FIXED: '.facts div' also matched '.facts > .wrap', which is a div and a first child, so
  the strip's cell padding overrode the page gutter. The four facts sat 18px left of every
  other section at phone widths and 4px right of it on desktop. All cell rules now use
  '.facts .wrap > div'.
- FIXED: '.nav + .head-call{margin-left:6px}' applied at every width, because an adjacent
  sibling selector does not care that '.nav' is display:none. Below 900px nothing carried
  margin-left:auto, so the call button and the menu button bunched against the wordmark
  with the right half of the header empty. The auto now lives on '.head-call' and only
  moves to the nav at 900px.
- FIXED: the gallery left two dead 269x220 cells in the second row above 900px. Four
  items with span-2 and span-2-rows auto-flowed into a 4x2 grid and could not fill it.
  Placement is now explicit: the floor 2x2, the bar 1x2, the other two stacked at the right.
- FIXED: three offer chips overflowed their column. The chip column is a fixed width so all
  five banners share a right edge, but the chip type was clamp(12px,1.05vw,14.5px) and at
  wide viewports '$1,000 year-end drawing' needed 254px of a 232px column. Chip type is a
  flat 13px and the column is 236px, 244px past 1200px.
- FIXED: the rules above the promo house rules and the footer legal block were drawn on the
  text itself, so they stopped at the 78ch measure and read as unfinished. They are now
  drawn on the block above, full container width, with the text still set narrow.
- Hero 'tonight' line pulled out of the hero and rebuilt as a full-bleed band between the
  hero and the facts strip, with a gradient hairline, the promo, the time, the countdown and
  a link into the week. It ships hidden and Site.js only reveals it once it has real content,
  so a weekend or a JS failure no longer paints a row of placeholder dashes.
- Hero height 92svh -> 86svh so that band breaks the fold and invites the scroll.
- Section rhythm centralised on --sec-pad / --sec-gap / --grid-gap instead of per-section
  numbers, and the two inline style attributes on #room and #club moved into .tight-top
  and .banded.
- Open/closed sign runs as a row above 560px instead of stacking, which buys the hero
  headline a line of vertical space. Headline set to text-wrap:balance so it no longer
  orphans 'up right.' on its own line.
- Facts strip, section heads, visit panel and footer reworked to use the full width:
  facts are flush to the container edges with hairline dividers, every section head is a
  headline-left / supporting-line-right row, the visit panel's hours read as a ruled
  spec table, and the footer is three columns (brand / nav / contact) instead of one
  narrow stack.
- Webfonts moved out of Aurora_Tokens.css into index.html with preconnect. An @import
  inside a stylesheet cannot start downloading until that stylesheet has parsed, which
  serialised two round trips before any text could paint. Unused weights dropped as well:
  Sora 400 and Manrope 500 were requested and never used.
- Added: skip link, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter tags, and Casino JSON-LD with the
  address, phone, hours and social profiles. Promo titles are h3 elements rather than divs,
  the hero is labelled by its own h1, and the menu button relabels itself when open.
- Removed dead CSS: .sr-only, .nav a.soon, .mobile-nav a span, and the ::selection and
  :focus-visible duplicates that Aurora_Tokens.css already binds. The shimmer stagger no
  longer injects a <style> element at runtime; the rule lives in Site.css and Site.js only
  sets --sweep.
- Site.css given a table of contents and section banners; Site.js given a header comment,
  a numbered run order and one EDIT ME block holding the hours and the whole week.
- Canonical, og:url, og:image and the JSON-LD url/image/logo now point at
  aurora-casino-site.web.app. auroracasinomt.com still resolves to the old Wix site, so
  pointing them there told search engines and every link preview that the real page was
  the Wix one. Flip all of them the day the domain cuts over.
- Archiving note: no copy was written to _version_archive/. The build root is itself the
  git repo, so a full tree copy would commit the 9.3 MB hero mp4 a second time on every
  bump. The outgoing version is tagged v0.0.1-alpha instead; `git worktree add` or
  `git archive` against that tag reproduces it byte for byte.
